# Changelog — OrbiTouch ATS

All notable changes to **OrbiTouch ATS** (OrbiTouch HR & Recruitment Consultants / Orbitouch Outsourcing Private Limited) are documented in this file.

## [1.1.1] - Production Conflict Prevention, Config-Cache Safety & Workflow Guardrails
### Fixed & Hardened
- **Production `config:cache` Compatibility:** Added `config/ats.php` and replaced all direct `env()` calls inside `app/` and `DatabaseSeeder` with `config('ats.*')` so production caching never resets 2FA enforcement, storage quotas, or external backup settings.
- **Collision-Free Entity Codes:** Added `CodeGeneratorService` across all 10 sequential business codes (`CLT`, `JOB`, `CND`, `APP`, `SUB`, `INT`, `OFR`, `JON`, `PLC`, `ORB-USR`, `TM`) to prevent duplicate-key errors after record deletions, year rollovers, or concurrent requests.
- **Cross-Team Duplicate Effort & Soft-Delete Recovery:** Hardened application, submission, interview conflict, and offer revision lookups to prevent cross-recruiter SQL unique-constraint crashes and cleanly restore soft-deleted applications.
- **Multi-Job Candidate Stage Accuracy & Notification URLs:** Updated `PipelineService` to preserve a candidate's highest active stage across concurrent jobs and point pipeline notifications to `/candidates/{id}` instead of non-existent `/applications/{id}` URLs.
- **Replacement Guardrails, Backup FK Ordering & Alert Deduplication:** Blocked premature replacements on unjoined records, removed unsafe `Placement::firstOrFail()` fallback, included `users`/`team_members` with foreign-key-safe restoration in `BackupService`, deduplicated daily storage/reminder alerts, and added inline Job & User account editing modals.

## [1.1.0] - Workspace Access Hardening, Reports/CSV Reliability, Verified 2FA & Light-Theme UI Polish
### Fixed & Improved
- **Candidate Optional Fields & Consent Default:** Added migration `0001_01_01_000009` making `notice_period_days`, `total_experience`, `relevant_experience`, `availability_status`, and submission experience/notice snapshot columns nullable; updated candidate creation, editing, and CSV import to default `consent_status` to `pending` (recording `consent_recorded_at` only when explicitly `granted`).
- **Consistent Report & CSV Export Filtering:** Unified filter validation and dataset queries (`from_date`, `to_date`, `recruiter_id`, `client_id`, `job_id`, `status`) across all 18 operational reports for both the web UI (`/reports`) and formula-safe CSV exports (`/reports/export`).
- **Idempotent Recurring Tasks:** Wrapped task completion in a `DB::transaction` with `lockForUpdate()` and `auto_trigger_key` deduplication so completing a recurring task multiple times or concurrently never generates duplicate follow-up tasks.
- **Strict Role & Tenant Data Isolation:** Enforced `RecruitmentAccess` query and eager-loading relation scopes plus `AuthorizeRecruitmentAccess` middleware across recruiters, teams, and managers; tightened `DocumentController` so clients can only download exact resume versions and client-visible documents explicitly shared with their organisation.
- **CSV Import/Export & Form Validation:** Added UTF-8 BOM stripping, `,`/`;` delimiter detection, formula-escape apostrophe unwrapping (`'+91...`), numeric column validation, human-readable error messages, and active-filter synchronization on candidate CSV export.
- **Verified Two-Factor Authentication Setup:** Required 6-digit TOTP code verification (`/profile/two-factor/confirm`) against a session-scoped pending setup secret before enabling 2FA; removed live TOTP previews and persistent secret/recovery code exposure from `/profile`, displaying recovery codes once upon confirmation or password-verified regeneration.
- **Light-Theme Workspace & Mobile/Desktop UI Polish:** Refined responsive sidebar collapse/drawer behavior, `Ctrl+K` global search badges, inline modal validation alerts, null notice-period formatting (`Notice N/A` / `Not provided`), and Candidate 360 inline profile editing.

## [1.0.0] - Complete Enterprise Recruitment CRM & ATS Release (Phases 1–9)
### Added
- **Authentication, 2FA & RBAC (`Phase 1`):** Email/password authentication, login rate limiting, 5-attempt lockout, login activity history, TOTP Two-Factor Authentication + recovery codes, session revocation, controlled user invitations, 5 core roles (`super_admin`, `manager`, `team_leader`, `recruiter`, `client`) with 34 server-enforced permissions, team management, and configurable business settings (`Mon–Sat 10:00 AM – 6:30 PM IST`).
- **Client & Job Requirement Management (`Phase 2`):** Centralised Client CRM with 10 live KPIs, multiple HR contacts, private agreement uploads, Job Requirement engine with vacancy counters (`openings_count`, `joined_count`, `remaining_count`), requirement duplication without duplicating applications, and primary/secondary recruiter assignment with instant notifications.
- **Candidate Master Database & Private Storage (`Phase 3`):** 360-degree candidate profiles, multi-signal duplicate detection (normalized mobile, normalized email, alternate phone, SHA-256 resume checksum), safe administrator duplicate merge, versioned private resume storage (`ResumeVersion`), side-panel quick preview drawer, and formula-safe CSV bulk import/export.
- **Calling, Screening, Submissions & Visual Pipeline (`Phase 4`):** Chronological call discussion logger, configurable job screening checklists, immutable client profile submission snapshots, bulk profile submission, duplicate submission prevention, and interactive 13-stage Kanban + Table pipeline with optimistic locking (`409 Conflict` protection) and automated follow-up task generation.
- **Unlimited Multiple Interview Rounds & Client Portal (`Phase 5`):** Unlimited configurable interview rounds per job (`1..N`), multiple reschedule attempt tracking (`InterviewAttempt`), conflict detection, failed-round progression protection with audited admin override, and dedicated tenant-isolated Client HR Portal (`/portal/*`) with side-by-side candidate comparison.
- **Offers, Joining, Placements & Replacements (`Phase 6`):** Offer management with salary revision history (`OfferRevision`), private offer letter storage, joining confirmation with atomic vacancy recalculation and duplicate placement prevention, post-joining retention check-ins (30/60/90 days), and replacement warranty tracking with authorised vacancy reopening.
- **Executive Dashboards, Productivity & 18 Reports (`Phase 7`):** Super Admin Executive Dashboard with documented conversion formulas and clickable drilldowns, Recruiter `My Work` workspace, Team/Client performance analytics, Global Command Palette (`Ctrl+K`), Tasks & Calendar, Notification Centre, and 18 exportable operational reports.
- **Production Reliability, Backups & Hostinger Deployment Package (`Phases 8–9`):** Hostinger 20 GB storage quota dashboard with 70%/85%/95% alerts, AES-256 encrypted daily backup & SHA-256 verified restoration engine, soft-deleted record recovery vault, System Health diagnostics, 30-scenario automated PHPUnit test suite, and zero-data-loss Hostinger deployment/rollback scripts.
